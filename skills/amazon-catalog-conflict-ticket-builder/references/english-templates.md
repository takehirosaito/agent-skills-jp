# 英語版テンプレート（グローバルカタログサポート向け）

日本のセラーサポートでは解決しない場合や、グローバル商標問題で英語ケースを開く際のテンプレート。

## 共通ガイドライン

- Subject line is one sentence with ASIN, problem type
- Open with conclusion (what you want changed)
- Use bullet points
- Attach supporting documents
- Keep under 600 words

---

## 1. Brand Name Correction

### Subject
```text
ASIN:B0XXXXXXXX Brand name correction request ([Current Brand] → [Correct Brand])
```

### Body
```text
Hello,

I am requesting a brand name correction for the following ASIN.

- ASIN: B0XXXXXXXX
- Current brand name: [Current Brand]
- Correct brand name: [Correct Brand]

Justification:
- We are the trademark holder of [Correct Brand] (Trademark Registration No. ○○, attached)
- We are enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry under the brand name [Correct Brand]
- This ASIN represents our genuine product distributed by our company

Attached documents:
1. Trademark registration certificate
2. Manufacturer authorization letter (if applicable)
3. Product packaging image showing brand logo

Please advise if additional documentation is needed.

Thank you.
```

## 3. Main Image Replacement

### Subject
```text
ASIN:B0XXXXXXXX Main image replacement request
```

### Body
```text
Hello,

I am requesting a main image replacement for the following ASIN.

- ASIN: B0XXXXXXXX
- Reason: The current main image is low quality and does not match the latest product version
- Brand Registry status: Enrolled (Brand: ○○)

Submitted images comply with Amazon main image guidelines:
- Pure white background RGB(255, 255, 255)
- Product fills at least 85% of the frame
- Minimum 1500×1500 px
- JPG format, no text/logo/border/props

Attached:
1. New main image (attached)
2. Additional product images (sub-images 1-7)

Thank you.
```

## 4. Counterfeit Reseller Report

### Subject
```text
ASIN:B0XXXXXXXX Counterfeit reseller report
```

### Body
```text
Hello,

I am reporting a counterfeit reseller on the following ASIN. Please investigate and remove the listing.

- ASIN: B0XXXXXXXX
- We are the authorized seller and Brand Registry holder (Brand: ○○)
- Reseller name / ID: [Seller name / ID]

Evidence:
- On 2025/XX/XX we made a test purchase from the reseller
- The received product is counterfeit (see attached authentication report)
- Packaging, logo placement, and serial number system differ from our genuine product

Attached documents:
1. Authentication report from our quality assurance department
2. Comparison images: genuine product vs. counterfeit
3. Test purchase order confirmation and product photos

We request the reseller's listing on this ASIN be removed, and a takedown via Brand Registry IPR.

Thank you.
```

## 5. ASIN Merge Request

### Subject
```text
Duplicate ASIN merge request (B0XXXXXXX, B0YYYYYYY → B0ZZZZZZZ)
```

### Body
```text
Hello,

I am requesting an ASIN merge for duplicate listings of the same physical product.

- Target ASIN (to retain): B0ZZZZZZZ
- Duplicate ASINs (to deactivate): B0XXXXXXX, B0YYYYYYY

Justification:
- All three ASINs represent the same physical product (JAN: same, manufacturer model: same)
- Having separate ASINs splits reviews and complicates inventory management

Attached documents:
1. Manufacturer specification sheet (proves identity)
2. JAN and manufacturer model number cross-reference

Please retain B0ZZZZZZZ with its reviews and inventory, and deactivate the other two.

Thank you.
```

## English templates 共通の注意

- "Best regards," "Thank you" のような結びは丁寧めに
- 個人名を出す場合はファーストネームのみ
- 日付は YYYY/MM/DD 形式
- 添付資料は英語版がベターだが、日本語版でも「日本国内の公的書類」として通用
- 商標登録証は J-PlatPat の英語版ダウンロード機能を活用
